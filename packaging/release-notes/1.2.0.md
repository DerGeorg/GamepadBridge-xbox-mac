## Xbox 360 controllers

GamepadBridge now drives the **Xbox 360 Wireless Receiver for Windows** too
(`045e:0719`; the `0291` and `02a9` versions are recognised as well, but
untested), next to the Xbox Wireless Adapter.
Up to four Xbox 360 wireless controllers, each a pad of its own, with the
Xbox button, rumble, and the ring showing the controller's slot — alongside
any Xbox One controllers, all at once.

Xbox 360 controllers pair with the receiver's own button, then the connect
button on top of the controller. The menu explains it: there is no way to
start that from the Mac.

## Steam and the Xbox 360 receiver

Steam for Mac has a driver of its own for this receiver and takes it when it
gets there first. The menu then says "Xbox 360 receiver in use by Steam…" and
explains what that means; quit Steam, and GamepadBridge takes the receiver
over by itself.

## Opens at login

GamepadBridge now starts with your Mac — which also means it gets to the
Xbox 360 receiver before Steam does. Switch it off with "Open at Login" in
the menu; it stays off.

## Smaller things

- The adapter's firmware is asked for when an adapter is first plugged in,
  not at launch. With only an Xbox 360 receiver you are never asked.
- "Pair a Controller" without an adapter no longer quits the app.
- Quitting while waiting for the adapter now shuts down properly.

## Install

```sh
brew tap dergeorg/tap https://gitlab.dergeorg.at/mac/homebrew-tap.git
brew trust --cask dergeorg/tap/gamepadbridge
brew install --cask gamepadbridge
```

Or download `GamepadBridge.dmg` below. Signed and notarized; requires
macOS 15 or newer.
